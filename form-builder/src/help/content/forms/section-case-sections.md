A **case** follows a person over several contacts: counselors open one when someone needs ongoing support, and link their contacts to it.

Case sections are **what counselors can add to a case**, e.g. notes, referrals, household members, incidents, documents. Each section has its own form.

## What you can change

- Add sections (**+ Add a section**), rename them, and change their order.
- Each section's form: open it from the menu on the left, or with **Edit the form**. See [Case section forms](help:section-case-section-forms).
- Sections that already exist can only be removed by the Aselo team, because counselors would stop seeing what was saved in them.
