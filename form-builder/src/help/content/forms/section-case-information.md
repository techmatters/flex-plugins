This form is about **the contact itself**: what it was about and what happened. Counselors fill it in as they finish, e.g. a summary of the conversation, the risk level, what they did and what was agreed.

It's on every contact, whoever got in touch.

## What you can change

- Add, change, reorder or remove questions: see [Add a question](help:add-a-question).
- Make questions required, and add guidance for counselors under them.

The summary can't be removed: Aselo needs it.

> Despite its name, it isn't about [cases](help:section-case-sections): it's part of every contact. Cases are opened separately, to follow someone over several contacts.
