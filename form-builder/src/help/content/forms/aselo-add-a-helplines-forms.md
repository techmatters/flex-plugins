Only Aselo staff add forms to the list; the helpline's staff then see them.

1. On the Forms page, in **Import a helpline's forms**, choose the helpline.
2. Leave **Branch** empty for master, or type a flex-plugins branch (it suggests them).
3. Click **Preview**, then **Import** the version you want.

It stays in the list until you click **Remove**. **Discard draft** only throws away the changes.
