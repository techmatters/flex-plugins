Some things are locked because contacts already saved, and your reports, depend on them: changing them could break those reports or lose information.

Locked for helpline staff:

- the name an existing field's answers are saved under (its question text can still change)
- existing issue category names, and buttons that close a contact without a form (silent, wrong number...)
- removing case sections that already exist
- fields Aselo needs, such as the call summary, or the service user's age and gender

If you need one of these changed, [leave a comment](help:flag-or-comment) on it for the Aselo team, saying what and why: they can change it safely.
