Submissions are posted in Slack, and come first in the Forms list.

1. Click **Review** next to the draft.
2. Click **Review & apply** and read the summary, **Fix these first** and **Notes for the Aselo team**.
3. Open the changed forms. Check saved names, private information (PII), reporting and prepopulation; **Try it out in Aselo** to fill them in.
4. Fix small things directly in the draft (it saves for everyone), or flag them for the helpline (**Needs changes**, for someone at the helpline).
5. Answer the helpline's questions (**Reply and resolve**).
6. Approve each form, or field, for Aselo as you check it.
7. When it's ready, publish it: see [Publish a draft](help:aselo-publish-a-draft).
