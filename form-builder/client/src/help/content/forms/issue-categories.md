Issue categories are what counselors pick to say what a contact was about, e.g. _Mental health › Anxiety_.

1. In the left menu, open **Contact forms › Issue categories**. **How it looks in Aselo** shows them as counselors see them.
2. Click **+ Add a category** at the bottom, and type its name. You can choose its colour.
3. Click its **subcategories** button to open it, then **+ Add a subcategory**, or **Paste several at once** (one per line) and **Add these**.
4. Each subcategory can have a **help link** for counselors (optional).
5. Drag categories and subcategories to change their order.
6. Set **How many can counselors choose for one contact?** at the top if needed.

Removing a category or subcategory asks first. Contacts already saved with it keep it; counselors just can't choose it anymore. Existing category names can't be renamed by helpline staff, because contacts and reports use them: [leave a comment](help:flag-or-comment) for the Aselo team if one must change.
