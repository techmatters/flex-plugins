Aselo uses a few words everywhere: **child**, **caller**, **case**, **client profile**, **counselor** and **contact**. Not every helpline uses these words, so you can choose your own here, and Aselo uses them on every tab, button, list and message counselors see.

For each word:

- Pick one of the usual answers, or **Something else** to type your own.
- Write it as you would in the middle of a sentence (“help seeker”, not “Help Seeker”): Aselo adds capitals where a heading or a button needs them.
- Check **Several**: it's filled in for you (“young person” becomes “young people”), but you can change it.
- **How counselors will see it** shows a few places it appears, with the word changed.

For a client profile there's a full name (“Client Profile”) and a short name, used on its own (“Edit Profile”).

> Some of your helpline's text may already have been written for you by the Aselo team. Those strings keep their own wording, and the section says how many there are for each word.

What this doesn't change:

- Your forms' own questions and answers: change them in each section.
- Messages in your contact flows, chatbots and webchat. Ask the Aselo team if those need your words too.

Your choices are saved in your draft like any other change, and reach counselors once your changes are approved and published.
