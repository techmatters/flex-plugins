Each [case section](help:section-case-sections) has a form: what counselors fill in when they add one to a case, e.g. a referral's organisation and date, or a household member's name and relationship.

## What you can change

Everything you can change on a contact form: add, change, reorder or remove questions, make them required, add guidance, and change dropdowns' answers. See [Add a question](help:add-a-question).

These forms are reviewed like the contact forms: approve them for your helpline, and leave questions or comments on them.
