These are the messages **Aselo sends to the person by itself** in chats, in each of your helpline's languages:

- **During the chat**: _Welcome_ (when a counselor accepts the conversation), _Goodbye_ (when the counselor ends it), _Contact ended the chat_, _Outside opening hours_, _Closed for a holiday_, and _Unsupported attachment_ (when someone sends something Aselo can't receive, like a sticker).
- **Post-chat survey**: the invitation to answer a short survey after the conversation, and the thank-you at the end.

## What you can change

The text of each message, in each language. Leave one empty to use Aselo's standard text.

Messages sent by your contact flows (e.g. the first message someone gets when they text you) aren't here: they're designed in **Contact flows**.
