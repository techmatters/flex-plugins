Each question counselors answer is a **field**. You can add one to any contact form tab or case form.

1. In the left menu, open the form, e.g. **Contact forms › Help seeker information**.
2. Click **+ Add a field** where you want it.
3. Choose **what kind of answer** it takes: **Short text** (a name, a city), **Long text** (notes), **Dropdown** (pick one from a list), **Multiple choice list**, **Checkbox**, **Yes / No / Unknown**, **Date**, **Time**, **Number**, **Email address**, **Radio buttons**... or **Linked dropdowns** (see [Set up linked dropdowns](help:linked-dropdowns)).
4. In **Field settings** on the right, write the **Question** as counselors should read it.
5. For a dropdown, add its answers (see [Change the answers of a dropdown](help:dropdown-answers)).
6. Optional: tick **Counselors must answer this** to make it required, and add a **Help box under the field** with guidance, e.g. how to ask a sensitive question.
7. Drag the field (by its handle) to move it up or down.
8. Click **Try it out in Aselo** to fill it in as a counselor would.

> **Good to know:** the builder gives each new field a name its answers are saved under. Once the field exists that name can't change (contacts and reports use it), but the question text can, any time.
