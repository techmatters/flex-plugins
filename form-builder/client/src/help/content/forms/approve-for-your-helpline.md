Each contact form tab and case form is approved by two sides: **your helpline** (is this what we want counselors to see?) and **Aselo** (is it technically right?). Anyone at your helpline can approve for it.

1. Open the form and read it through, or fill it in with **Try it out in Aselo**.
2. In the **Review** box at the top of the form, click **Approve** for your helpline. To approve just one field, click it: its Review box is above its settings.
3. The form gets a **blue ✓** (approved by your helpline). The Aselo team adds a **green ✓** when they've checked it.

A **grey ●** means it was approved, then changed: look at it again and approve it again. **Undo** takes your approval back.

> **Tip:** approving isn't the same as sending: when your team is happy with everything, [send your changes for review](help:submit-for-review).
