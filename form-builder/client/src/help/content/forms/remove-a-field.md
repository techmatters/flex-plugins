Click the field, then **Remove this field** at the bottom of its settings. The builder asks first, and tells you what else uses that field, e.g. your reports, the webchat form or linked dropdowns, so you can decide.

Some fields can't be removed because Aselo needs them (the call summary, the service user's age and gender). Removing a field only affects new contacts: contacts already saved keep their answers.
