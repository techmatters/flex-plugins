This form is about the **person who got in touch** when they're contacting on behalf of someone else: a parent worried about their child, a teacher, a friend. Counselors fill it in with what they learn about that person: name, age, relationship to the service user, where they are...

Counselors only see this tab when the call type says the contact is about someone else (e.g. *Someone calling about a child*). When people contact you about themselves, their details go in [Service user information](help:section-service-user-information) instead.

## What you can change

- Add, change, reorder or remove questions: see [Add a question](help:add-a-question).
- Make questions required, and add guidance for counselors under them.
- Change the answers of its dropdowns: see [Change the answers of a dropdown](help:dropdown-answers).

> **In Aselo** this tab may still be called *Caller information* for your counselors: the builder uses “help seeker” because not every helpline takes calls.
