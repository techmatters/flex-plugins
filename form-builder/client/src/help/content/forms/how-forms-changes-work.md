You and your team decide what counselors see; the Aselo team checks it works technically and publishes it. Nothing reaches counselors until then, so you can try things out safely.

1. **You open your helpline's forms** from the list on the Forms page. See [Open your helpline's forms](help:open-your-forms).
2. **You make your changes**: add or change questions, answers, categories, messages... Everything saves by itself into a **draft** shared by your team.
3. **You and your team review them together.** Leave questions or comments on any form or field, and approve the forms that are right for your helpline. See [Ask a question or leave a comment](help:flag-or-comment) and [Approve a form for your helpline](help:approve-for-your-helpline).
4. **You send the draft for review** with **Review & submit**. See [Send your changes for review](help:submit-for-review).
5. **The Aselo team reviews it**: they check the technical side (how answers are saved, private information, reports), answer your questions, and may fix small things or ask you for changes.
6. **If they ask for changes, you make them and submit again.** See [When the Aselo team asks for changes](help:when-changes-are-asked).
7. **The Aselo team publishes it.** Counselors see the changes once it's live, and the draft is cleared.

> **Tip:** questions you leave in the draft are the quickest way to reach the Aselo team about a form: they see them while reviewing.
