Your helpline's forms are listed on the **Forms** page, ready to open.

1. Click **Forms** in the top bar.
2. Find your helpline in the list. **Status** says where it is: _No changes yet_, _In progress_ (someone is changing it) or _Submitted for review_.
3. Click **Open**.
4. Use the menu on the left to choose what to change: **Contact forms**, **Chats and calls**, **Cases** and **Client profiles** (the last two only if your helpline uses them).
5. To go back to the list, click **All forms** at the top.

> **Nothing in the list?** The Aselo team adds each helpline's forms to it. Email support@aselo.org and ask them to add yours.
