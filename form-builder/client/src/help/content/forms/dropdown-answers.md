A dropdown's answers are edited in its **Field settings**.

1. Open the form and click the dropdown's field.
2. Under **Answers**, change any answer's text, or click **+ Add an answer** at the bottom.
3. To add many at once, click **Paste several at once** and paste them, one per line. Two columns copied from a spreadsheet work too.
4. Drag answers to change their order; remove one with its ×.
5. Check it with **Try it out in Aselo**.

> **Renaming an answer** changes what counselors see, not what's saved: contacts already saved with it keep matching it, so reports don't break. See [What's an answer's “saved value”?](help:saved-values).

The first, empty answer of a dropdown is how Aselo shows “not answered yet”: leave it there.
