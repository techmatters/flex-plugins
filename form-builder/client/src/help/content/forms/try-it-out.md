You can fill in the draft form exactly as counselors would, before anything is published.

1. Open the form.
2. Click **Try it out in Aselo**. The form opens full-screen, as in the counselor's screen.
3. Fill it in: try the dropdowns, the required fields, the linked dropdowns.
4. Close it to go back to editing.

Nothing you type in it is saved: it's only for trying. Next to most editors there's also a small preview that updates as you change things.
