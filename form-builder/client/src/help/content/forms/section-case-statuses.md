A case's status says **where it's up to**, e.g. _Open_, _In progress_, _Closed_. Counselors change it from the case, choosing among the statuses it's allowed to move to.

## What you can change

- Add statuses, rename them and change their order.
- Which statuses each one can move to.

_Open_ (new cases) and _Closed_ are needed by Aselo and can't be removed.
