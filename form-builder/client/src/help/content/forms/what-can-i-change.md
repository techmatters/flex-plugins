The left menu has everything you can change:

- **Contact forms**: the contact form tabs (e.g. help seeker and service user information) and the **Issue categories**.
- **Chats and calls**: **Call types**, **Canned responses**, **Automatic messages** and the **Webchat form** (what people fill in before a chat).
- **Cases**: case **Sections**, **Case statuses**, and each section's form, if your helpline uses cases.
- **Client profiles**, if your helpline uses them.

Next to most editors there's a preview of how counselors see it.
