After you submit, the Aselo team reviews the draft. They may fix small things themselves, answer your questions, or ask you for changes.

1. On the Forms page, your draft's **Status** says what's open, e.g. _1 open question_ or _2 changes needed_.
2. Open it. Forms and fields with open flags have an icon, in the left menu and on the field.
3. Read each flag, make the change, and **Reply and resolve** it (e.g. “Done, renamed”). Answer questions the same way.
4. When everything is done, [send it for review](help:submit-for-review) again.

Once the Aselo team approves and publishes the draft, counselors see the changes and the draft is cleared.

> **Want to know where it is?** Email support@aselo.org with your helpline's name.
