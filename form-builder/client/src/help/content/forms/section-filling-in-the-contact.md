Which parts of the contact form Aselo fills in from the chatbot and webchat form answers, and which call type it picks: *Always the same forms*, or *Depends on an answer* (an ordered list of chatbot or webchat answers, and the value that means “about themselves”).

It's the `formSelector` of `PrepopulateMappings.json`, and only applies when `use_prepopulate_mappings` is on (Helpline › Features).
