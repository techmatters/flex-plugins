1. Open the Chatbots page and choose your helpline's chatbot on the left (each language is its own chatbot).
2. In **Conversation**, the whole chat is shown in order, from how it starts to the goodbye. Click any text to change it.
3. Move questions up or down, add new ones or remove them.
4. For each question, choose its **Answers**: a list (e.g. *Yes* / *No*, with other ways people say them, like *yeah*), a pattern (e.g. a 5-digit zip code), or a ready-made kind (a number, a date, a US state...).
5. **Fills in on the contact form** says which contact form field the answer goes into.
6. Try it in the **phone preview** on the right: tap or type answers, and see what the counselor would get at the end.

> **Tip:** add the ways people really write their answers to a list (*si*, *sí*, *yes*, *y*): the chatbot only understands what's listed.

Comments can be left on the chatbot, on each question and on each answer list. The **Check** lists anything missing, e.g. a question without text or an answer that means two things.
