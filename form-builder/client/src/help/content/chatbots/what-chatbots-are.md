A chatbot is **an automated conversation** with someone who messages your helpline, usually a few questions before they reach a counselor (a pre-survey: age, what they want to talk about...) or after (a post-survey about how it went).

Their answers can fill in the contact form, so the counselor doesn't ask again, and can decide where the contact goes. Each chatbot is one language.

In this section you design what each chatbot says and asks, and which answers it understands. The Aselo team builds it into your helpline once it's agreed.
