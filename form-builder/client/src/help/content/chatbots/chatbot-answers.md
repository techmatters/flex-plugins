A chatbot only understands the answers its question's list includes (and the other ways of saying them), or what matches its pattern. Anything else gets the retry message, and after the last try, the give-up message.

To fix it, open the question's **Answers** and add the way people wrote it, e.g. _yep_ to _Yes_. Each language's chatbot has its own lists, so a Spanish chatbot needs Spanish ways of saying them. Try it in the phone preview.
