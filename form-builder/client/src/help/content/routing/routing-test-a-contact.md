**Test a task** shows where a contact would go, without anyone getting in touch.

1. Open Routing and your helpline's workflow.
2. In **Test a task**, click what the contact looks like: its channel (call, chat...), its service, its language. More details, like transfers, are under *More details*.
3. Read what happens: the rule that takes it, the queue, which counselors, what happens if nobody takes it, and why the rules before it didn't take it.

If it goes somewhere unexpected, look at the rules before it: the first one that fits wins.
