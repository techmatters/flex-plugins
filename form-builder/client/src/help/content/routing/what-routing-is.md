Routing decides **which counselors get each contact**, once it's waiting in line.

- **Queues** are waiting lines for groups of counselors, e.g. general, Spanish, youth line.
- **Rules** (in a **workflow**) look at the contact (its channel, service, language...) and send it to a queue, in order: the first rule that fits takes it.
- **Skills** say what each counselor can take, e.g. _Spanish_ or _Calls_. A rule can ask for some skills, prefer some counselors, and say what happens if nobody takes the contact in time.

The diagram next to the rules draws it like a flowchart. Changes are designed here and built into your helpline by the Aselo team.
