Steps are added from the menu on the left and joined with arrows.

1. Drag a step from the left menu onto the board, inside the frame of the flow it belongs to, or click it to add it in the middle of the view. Hover a step in the menu to see what it does.
2. Double-click its text to write what it is, e.g. _Welcome message_.
3. To connect two steps, drag from the edge of the first one and let go over the second. The arrow is labelled with the **way out** it is, e.g. _open_ or _closed_.
4. Each way out leads to one place. A step with several (a Decision, Check operating hours...) gets one arrow per way out.
5. Select a step to see its settings on the right. The (i) on a step explains it, with examples.
6. To remove a step, select it and press Delete. **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) brings it back.

> **Tip:** every way out should lead somewhere: a contact on a way out that isn't connected is dropped. The **Check** tab lists any that aren't.
