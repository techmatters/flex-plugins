Every message on a board has a **script**: its exact words. For calls, the script is recorded and the recording is uploaded.

1. Switch to **Build** mode and select the message.
2. Write its **script** in its settings, in its language.
3. For a call (voice) message, upload the recording in its **Recording** section with **Upload recording** (MP3 or WAV). The **Recordings** tab can upload several at once: each file is matched to its message by its name.
4. The Aselo team listens to it and approves it, then publishes it for your live line.

A message's status moves along by itself: *No script* → *Script ready* → *Recorded* (calls) → *Approved* → *Published* (calls). An approved message has a green tick on the board.

> **Until a call message is recorded**, a computer voice reads its script, so the flow can be tested early. Uploading a new take sends it back for approval; your live line keeps the old one until then.
