Yes: the board saves by itself after each change.

Several people can work on it, one at a time: if someone else saved while you were editing, you're asked to reload, so nobody's work is overwritten without noticing. Comments and tasks are a good way to work on it together without getting in each other's way.

The **Recordings** button in the toolbar reloads the recordings, to see what others uploaded since you opened the board.
