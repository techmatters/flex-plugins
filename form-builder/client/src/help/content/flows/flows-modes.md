Three modes in the toolbar, in the order a flow is made:

- **Sketch**: the flow's shape only: its steps, names and paths. Steps look lighter, with dashed outlines, and scripts and settings are hidden (not lost).
- **Build**: every detail: scripts, recordings, settings.
- **Test**: walks through the flow; the board can't be changed. See [Test a flow](help:flows-test-a-flow).

If you can't move or change anything, you're probably in **Test** mode, or the board says _View only_.
