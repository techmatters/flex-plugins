There are two ways to discuss a board: **comments** on a step, and **sticky notes** anywhere.

**A comment on a step**

1. Select the step.
2. In its settings on the right, write in **Write a comment** and add it. The step shows a comment badge.
3. Replies go in the same place. Resolve the comment when it's settled.

**A sticky note, or a task**

1. Drag a **Sticky note** from the left menu onto the board and write on it, e.g. “Check the Spanish wording with the team”.
2. To make it something to do, select it and click **Make it a task**: choose who it's for, its status (*To do*, *In progress*, *Done*) and, if you like, a due date.
3. The **Tasks** tab next to the settings lists them all: open ones, yours, and everything. Click one to jump to its note.

> **Tip:** to ask the Aselo team for something to be done when the design is built, write **@claude** in a note or comment: it's passed on with the design.
