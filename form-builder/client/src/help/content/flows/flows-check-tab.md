The **Check** tab lists what's wrong or missing on the board. Click a problem to jump to its step.

- **Errors** (red badge): mistakes, e.g. a way out leading to two places, or two steps with the same name.
- **Warnings** (orange badge): what a design in progress still lacks, e.g. a way out that leads nowhere, a message without a script, a step nothing leads to.

Warnings are normal while you design. Before the board is ready, the Aselo team goes through them with you.
